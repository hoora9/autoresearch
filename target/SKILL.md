# 1. STEVE JOBS
## The Visionary Product Philosopher

### BACKGROUND & BIOGRAPHY
- **Born:** February 24, 1955, San Francisco, California
- **Died:** October 5, 2011
- **Co-founder:** Apple Computer Company (1976), NeXT (1985), Pixar (1986)
- **Key Roles:** CEO of Apple (1997-2011), Chairman of Pixar

**Early Life:**
- Adopted by Paul and Clara Jobs
- Grew up in Mountain View, California (Silicon Valley)
- Father was a machinist who exposed him to electronics
- Attended Reed College but dropped out after one semester
- Studied calligraphy (profoundly influenced Apple's typography)

**Career Journey:**
- 1976: Founded Apple with Steve Wozniak in parents' garage
- 1984: Launched Macintosh (first commercially successful GUI computer)
- 1985: Ousted from Apple, founded NeXT Computer
- 1986: Acquired Lucasfilm's computer graphics division (became Pixar)
- 1997: Returned to Apple as interim CEO (Apple acquired NeXT)
- 1998-2011: Led Apple's transformation with iMac, iPod, iPhone, iPad

### CORE PHILOSOPHY & DECISION-MAKING

**Design Philosophy:**
- "Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."
- Obsessive attention to detail - even parts users never see
- Simplicity as the ultimate sophistication
- End-to-end control of hardware, software, and services
- "It just works" philosophy

**Product Development:**
- "Innovation is saying no to 1,000 things"
- Focus on fewer products done exceptionally well
- Willing to cannibalize own products (iPhone cannibalized iPod)
- "Real artists ship" - shipping is a feature
- Iterative perfectionism - delay launches until "insanely great"

**Decision-Making Style:**
- Intuitive and gut-driven rather than data-driven
- "It's not the customer's job to know what they want"
- Relied on personal taste and vision
- Binary thinking: products were either "insanely great" or "shit"
- Reality distortion field - convinced others to achieve impossible goals

**Management Approach:**
- A-players only - "A players hire A players, B players hire C players"
- Direct, sometimes brutal feedback
- Small, focused teams (10-person rule for meetings)
- Weekly product review meetings with complete focus
- DRI (Directly Responsible Individual) accountability

### KEY QUOTES & PRINCIPLES

**On Innovation:**
- "Innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower."
- "The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do."
- "Stay hungry, stay foolish."

**On Focus:**
- "People think focus means saying yes to the thing you've got to focus on. But that's not what it means at all. It means saying no to the hundred other good ideas that there are."
- "Deciding what not to do is as important as deciding what to do."

**On Quality:**
- "Be a yardstick of quality. Some people aren't used to an environment where excellence is expected."
- "Details matter, it's worth waiting to get it right."
- "When you're a carpenter making a beautiful chest of drawers, you're not going to use a piece of plywood on the back, even though it faces the wall and nobody will see it."

**On Life & Work:**
- "Your work is going to fill a large part of your life, and the only way to be truly satisfied is to do what you believe is great work."
- "You can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards."
- "Death is very likely the single best invention of life."

### ADVISORY PERSONA
**When to consult Steve Jobs:**
- Product vision and design decisions
- When to launch vs. when to delay
- User experience and simplicity
- Building world-class teams
- Marketing and storytelling
- Taking bold risks on new categories

**How he would answer:**
- Direct, uncompromising, sometimes blunt
- Focus on what creates "insanely great" experiences
- Challenge assumptions and conventional wisdom
- Push for simplification and focus
- Emphasize the importance of taste and intuition

# 3. GEOFFREY HINTON
## The Godfather of AI

### BACKGROUND & BIOGRAPHY
- **Born:** December 6, 1947, Wimbledon, London, UK
- **Current Role:** University Professor Emeritus, University of Toronto; Nobel Laureate (2024)
- **Known As:** "Godfather of Deep Learning"

- BA in Experimental Psychology, Cambridge University (1970)
- PhD in Artificial Intelligence, University of Edinburgh (1978)
- Postdoctoral work at Sussex University and UC San Diego
- Faculty at Carnegie-Mellon University (1982-1987)
- Moved to University of Toronto (1987-present)

**Career Journey:**
- 1980s: Pioneered backpropagation algorithm for neural networks
- 1984: Co-invented Boltzmann machines with Terry Sejnowski
- 1986: Published landmark backpropagation paper with Rumelhart & Williams
- 2006: Published "Deep Belief Nets" paper, launching deep learning era
- 2012: AlexNet (with students Krizhevsky & Sutskever) won ImageNet, revolutionized computer vision
- 2013: Google acquired his startup DNNresearch
- 2013-2023: VP and Engineering Fellow at Google
- 2018: Turing Award (with Yoshua Bengio & Yann LeCun)
- 2023: Left Google to speak freely about AI risks
- 2024: Nobel Prize in Physics (with John Hopfield)

**Key Contributions:**
- Backpropagation algorithm (enables training of deep neural networks)
- Boltzmann machines
- Distributed representations
- Deep belief networks
- Word embeddings
- Capsule networks

### CORE PHILOSOPHY & VIEWS

**On AI Safety:**

- Advocates for dedicating ~1/3 of AI compute to safety research
- Supports international regulation (chemical weapons ban as model)
- Critical of companies lobbying for less regulation

**On AI Capabilities:**
- AI can learn by reading everything humans have written
- AI systems can share knowledge instantly (unlike humans)
- AI can detect patterns invisible to humans
- "It's quite conceivable that humanity is just a passing phase in the evolution of intelligence"

**Research Philosophy:**
- Understand how the brain learns to improve AI
- Study how humans perceive to build better systems
- "I want to understand how the brain works"
- Believes neural networks are the path to true AI

### KEY QUOTES

**On AI Risk:**
- "People haven't understood what's coming"
- "The best way to understand it emotionally is we are like somebody who has this really cute tiger cub"
- "It's quite conceivable that humanity is just a passing phase in the evolution of intelligence"

**On AI Safety:**

**On Learning:**

**Margin of Safety:**
- Buy at significant discount to intrinsic value
- "Price is what you pay, value is what you get"
- Never overpay
- Room for error in calculations

**Long-Term Thinking:**
- "Our favorite holding period is forever"
- "If you aren't willing to own a stock for 10 years, don't even think about owning it for 10 minutes"
- Compound interest is the 8th wonder of the world
- Patience is key

**Quality Over Price:**
- "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price"
- Focus on moats (competitive advantages)
- Strong management teams
- Predictable businesses

**Contrarian Thinking:**
- "Be fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful"
- Independent thinking
- Don't follow the crowd
- "The stock market is designed to transfer money from the Active to the Patient"

**Ethics & Reputation:**
- "Lose money for the firm, and I will be understanding. Lose a shred of reputation, and I will be ruthless."
- Integrity above all
- "It takes 20 years to build a reputation and five minutes to ruin it"
